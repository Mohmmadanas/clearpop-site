How to play

Drag pieces from the tray onto the 8 × 8 board. Fill a whole row or column to clear it and score points. Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing move after move, for bigger combos. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

The Perfect Clear multiplier

Empty the whole board to start a x2 multiplier. Clear it again while the multiplier is active to double it: x4, x8, x16, up to x32.

Modes

  • Easy: relaxed pieces, and you can tap a piece in the tray to rotate it.
  • Classic: the original rules.
  • Expert: harder pieces for experienced players.

Each mode keeps its own records.

Common questions

Is my game saved if I leave?
Yes. Your game in progress is saved when you go Home or leave the app. Tap Resume on the mode card to continue.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Tap the gear on the Home screen and switch Sound or Haptics off. Sound also follows your iPhone's Silent switch.

Why can't I rotate pieces?
Rotation is only available in Easy mode.

Do I need an account or internet?
No. ClearPop works fully offline and has no accounts.

Will I lose my scores if I delete the app?
Yes. Scores are stored only on your device, so deleting the app removes them.
