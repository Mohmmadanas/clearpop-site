ClearPop

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 October 2026 · ClearPop is made by M.Anas Ali

Short version: ClearPop collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the game never connects to the internet. Your scores and settings stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. ClearPop doesn't collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It contains no third-party code (no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools) and makes no network connections.

What stays on your device

To work, the game saves a few things locally on your iPhone:

  • Your settings (sound and haptics on or off).
  • Your records for each mode: best score, highest multiplier, Perfect Clear count and games played.
  • A game in progress, so you can resume it.

This information never leaves your device through ClearPop and we can't see it. Like other app data, it may be included in your iCloud or computer backups, which Apple's own privacy terms cover. Deleting the app deletes it.

Sharing your score

If you tap Share Score, ClearPop opens the standard iOS share sheet with a short text about your score. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with, and then that app's own privacy policy applies. ClearPop doesn't receive or keep anything you share.

Tracking

ClearPop doesn't track you across apps or websites and doesn't use the advertising identifier.

Children

ClearPop isn't directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of ClearPop changes what it does with data, for example by adding ads, this policy will be updated on this page before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open a request on the ClearPop support page on GitHub.
